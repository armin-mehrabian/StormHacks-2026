# EscapeRoom A — Roadmap

Goal: win the ElevenLabs track with a game where **every sound is made by ElevenLabs and the room reacts to you**, on top of Gemini-generated rooms.

Each phase ends with a playable, demo-ready build and an owner review before the next phase starts.

| # | Phase | Scope | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sound foundation | Web Audio mixer (voice / music / ambience / SFX buses) with ducking under speech; one-time ElevenLabs SFX library script; looping ambience (rain, house creaks, ticking clock, lamp hum) placed in the room; footsteps; a search sound per object kind; lock, page, item, door, tension, and ending sounds; thunder with lamp flicker | 3 h |
| 2 | Narrator voice | Voice Design (owner picks from previews); Eleven v3 audio tags for big moments; word-synced subtitles via timestamps; personality arc (frustration score shapes tone) | 2.5 h |
| 3 | Sound puzzles | New objects: radio (numbers station), answering machine (voicemail), music box, chiming clock; an "audio clue" puzzle type the validator understands; on-screen transcripts | 3 h |
| 4 | Demo mode, Gemini rooms, themes | `?demo` fixed rehearsed room; `/api/room` generation with prefetch; 3 themes (palette, ambience, narrator flavour); Gemini-written per-object sound prompts generated by ElevenLabs and cached | 4 h |
| 5 | Reactive soundtrack | Calm / tense / panic music levels crossfaded on the timer; victory and failure stings | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Performance review | Run stats; Gemini recap performed with v3; downloadable roast card | 2 h |
| 7 | Directional whispers | Hints whispered from the target's direction; heartbeat that speeds up near the hidden key | 1 h |
| 8 | Fourth wall, languages, accessibility | Tab-switch and idle jokes; language picker; Q describes surroundings | 2 h |
| 9 | Mixer and sound credits | Per-bus volume sliders; end-screen list of every ElevenLabs sound used and its prompt | 1 h |
| 10 | Ship | README / Devpost, rehearse the 60-second demo, warm-up routine | 1.5 h |

**Core if time runs short:** 1, 2, 3, 4 (demo mode only), 6. Then 7 → 5 → 9 → 4 (generation and themes) → 8.

## 60-second demo script

1. Title screen: the designed narrator voice delivers the intro over the soundtrack.
2. Open the drawer three times: roasted, with word-synced subtitles.
3. Turn on the radio: a voice reads the code through static; enter it, the lock clicks open.
4. Escape: the narrator performs the performance review; the roast card appears.
5. "Every sound you heard was generated by ElevenLabs, and the room was designed by Gemini."
